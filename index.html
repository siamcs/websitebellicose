About Us

A Supply Partner for Diverse Operational Needs.

Consent Merchant is a Bangladesh-based general supplier, importer and contractor serving a broad range of institutional and commercial requirements.

Our Journey

Building a trusted supply partnership

Key milestones in our growth as a reliable institutional procurement partner.

2019

Company Established

Consent Merchant founded in Dhaka, Bangladesh, focusing on institutional equipment supply for government agricultural programs.

2020

First Major BADC Consignment

Successfully delivered 40 deep tube-well pump sets to Bangladesh Agricultural Development Corporation, clearing Chittagong Seaport ahead of schedule.

2021

Solar & Electrical Division Launched

Expanded into solar PV, inverters and high-voltage electrical equipment. First BREB solar substation component tender won.

2022

Humanitarian Relief & UN Partnerships

Entered food & relief supply sector, supporting WFP and UNHCR food parcel programs. ISO 22000 aligned supply chain established.

2023–24

PPE Division & Global OEM Network

Added certified PPE & safety equipment division. Built a network of 28+ verified international OEM partners across Europe and East Asia.

2025+

Digital Platform & Vision 2030

Launching smart port telemetry integration, AI tender matching engine and green procurement ESG framework for the next era of institutional trade.

Operational Coverage

Our footprint across Bangladesh

Key ports, delivery hubs and institutional client concentrations across the country.

🚢 Chittagong Seaport
Primary import gateway. Container terminal with direct customs house access. Average clearance velocity: 3.4 days. Handles 90%+ of our ocean freight consignments.
⚓ Mongla Seaport
Second largest port, ideal for bulk cargo, oversized machinery and project cargo. Direct river access to Khulna distribution zone.
🏙️ Dhaka — Head Office & Airport
Institutional client hub (BADC, BREB, DGDP, DNCC). Hazrat Shahjalal Int'l Airport handles urgent air freight consignments within 7–10 days.
🌊 Matarbari Deep Sea Hub (Phase 4 — 2029)
Future bonded transit hub, OEM factory stock holding, and emergency rapid deployment depot at Matarbari Deep Seaport.
